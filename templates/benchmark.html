Why PAC Outperforms Static Estimators:

The Passive-Aggressive algorithm updates model weights \(w\) only when an incoming sample incurs a non-zero hinge loss: L(w; (x, y)) = \max(0, 1 - y \cdot (w \cdot x)). When prediction is correct, it remains strictly passive; when misclassified, it updates aggressively with minimal perturbation, making it exceptionally resilient to shifting disinformation semantics.
