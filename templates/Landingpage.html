Core Innovation

The Problem & Our Source Weighting Approach

Most fake news detectors analyze articles in total isolation, leaving them vulnerable to single-article misclassifications. FAKEBUSTER shifts the paradigm toward Source Credibility Identification.

⚠️

Traditional Article Classification

Treats every piece of content in a vacuum. A single ambiguous or satiric article can yield a false positive, frustrating legitimate authors while nimble disinformation operations constantly cycle through disposable domains.

🎯

Source-Level Aggregation

By monitoring the track record of publishing sources over multiple articles, we broaden our article misclassification tolerance. Once a source exhibits a systematic pattern of producing fake news, its content visibility is throttled across social feeds.

Algorithmic Architecture

Why the Passive Aggressive Classifier?

The Passive Aggressive Classifier (PAC) is a margin-based online learning algorithm specifically tailored for massive, evolving text data.

01

Passive when Correct

If an incoming news article is accurately classified beyond the margin threshold (hinge loss = 0), the model weights remain untouched, preserving training stability.

02

Aggressive when Mistaken

If an article is misclassified, the algorithm executes an aggressive update with minimal perturbation, adapting to evolving disinformation terminology in real-time.

03

Production Serialization

The vectorizer and model weights are packaged as vector.pkl and model.pkl for ultra-low latency microsecond inference in Flask.
